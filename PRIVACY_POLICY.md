# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** February 21, 2026

## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our automated video publishing application ("App").

## 2. Data We Collect
- **TikTok OAuth Token**: We collect and store your TikTok access token to authenticate API requests on your behalf.
- **Video Metadata**: We process video file names, titles, and descriptions you provide for publication.
- **Limited User Info**: We retrieve basic user information (username, account ID) from TikTok API to verify account ownership.

## 3. How We Use Your Data
- To publish videos to your TikTok account as instructed.
- To authenticate API requests using OAuth tokens.
- To log errors and operational metrics for debugging.

## 4. Data Storage
- Tokens are stored locally on your machine or CI/CD runner (GitHub Actions).
- We do not transmit, sell, or share your data with third parties.
- Authentication tokens are kept private and not logged publicly.

## 5. Data Security
- OAuth tokens are handled securely using environment variables.
- We recommend rotating tokens regularly and revoking access if compromised.
- This App does not store personal data on external servers.

## 6. Your Rights
- You can revoke app permissions at any time in your TikTok account settings.
- You can request token regeneration to invalidate old credentials.

## 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy as needed. Updates will be effective upon posting.

## 8. Contact
For privacy questions, contact the app maintainer directly.
