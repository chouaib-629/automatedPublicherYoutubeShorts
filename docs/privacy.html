Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 21, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our automated video publishing application ("App").

2. Data We Collect

  • TikTok / YouTube OAuth tokens: We use access and refresh tokens to authenticate API requests on your behalf.
  • Video metadata: We process video file names, titles, and descriptions for publication.
  • Limited user info: We may retrieve basic user information from platform APIs to verify account ownership.

3. How We Use Your Data

To publish videos to your accounts as instructed, to authenticate API requests, and to log errors for debugging.

4. Data Storage

Tokens are stored locally on your machine or in your CI/CD runner (e.g. GitHub Actions). We do not transmit, sell, or share your data with third parties.

5. Data Security

OAuth tokens are handled via environment variables and secrets. We recommend rotating tokens regularly. This App does not store personal data on external servers.

6. Your Rights

You can revoke app permissions at any time in your TikTok or YouTube account settings and request token regeneration to invalidate old credentials.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as needed. Updates will be effective upon posting.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app maintainer directly.
